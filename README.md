A two-sentence horror story told through a domestic detail gone wrong: every jar in the pantry is labelled in the narrator's wife's handwriting — until a new jar appears, labelled "DADDY", and it is still warm. The second sentence reframes the first: a loving household chore becomes preparation, and the horror is left entirely implied.
